Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

My Dictionary ("the app") is a personal vocabulary app developed by Muhammad Tahir. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Information the app accesses

  • Google account information: when you sign in with Google, the app receives your name and email address to identify your account on your device.
  • Words you add: the words, meanings, synonyms and example sentences you enter. They are stored on your device.
  • Google Drive backup file: the app requests the drive.appdata permission. It can only read and write a single backup file in a hidden app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. It cannot see or access any of your other Drive files.

How the data is used

Your data is used only to run the app: to sign you in, to show your dictionary, and to back up and restore it to your own Google Drive. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties. We do not show ads and do not use analytics or tracking.

Storage and deletion

Your words are stored on your device and in your Google Drive app-data folder. You can delete them by uninstalling the app, deleting words in the app, and removing the app's data from Google Drive (Drive settings → Manage apps). You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
