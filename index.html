My Dictionary

My Dictionary is a personal vocabulary app for Android. Add the English words you learn each day, with their English meaning, Urdu meaning, synonyms and example sentences, and build your own dictionary.

Features

  • Sign in with your Google account
  • Save words with English and Urdu meanings, synonyms and multiple example sentences
  • Search your words, works offline
  • Automatic daily backup to your own Google Drive, and restore on a new phone
